Buyer groups, from 200 featured reviews on the site's Loox wall:

1. **Gave up on earrings for years.** The dominant story. "For the first time in 30 years, I can wear pierced earrings again."
2. **Tried everything, metals still hurt.** Titanium, surgical steel, nickel free, white gold, clear nail polish on the posts. "I even tried titanium, but my ears broke out almost immediately."
3. **Parents of kids with sensitive ears,** often moms who then buy for themselves. "I bought myself a pair as well because I have the same issues as my daughter."
4. **Collectors, teachers and themed wearers.** Pencils, music notes, holidays, sports, and a large Classical Conversations (home school) range. "I find myself choosing outfits around what I can wear these earrings."
5. **Gift buyers,** including partners. Digital gift cards exist for "Let them pick!"

Words customers use: wear earrings again, years, itchy, red, burning, crusty, holes closing, lightweight, don't even know they're on, cute, compliments, collection.

Voice: warm, family, a little goofy and punny ("We've got your back (ear)"), never clinical, never preachy. Short sentences. Faith and family appear naturally on the site; do not add them where the owner has not.

**Why:** ads and posts that sound like the customers convert better than polished jewelry copy.

**How to apply:** quote reviews only with first name and last initial as shown on the site, and only from the site. Pick one buyer group per ad. See [[no-medical-claims]].

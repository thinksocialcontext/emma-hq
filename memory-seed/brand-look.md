* Logo: wordmark "EARRINGS BY EMMA" in a condensed bold sans, hot pink #FD0082 with "BY" in teal #2FBAD1. A white stacked version exists for dark grounds.
* Accents: deep purple #5E106B, lavender #8B85C1, soft cream tints.
* Fonts: Fjalla One for headings, Poppins for body (both free Google Fonts).
* Photos: product cutouts on pure white with the clear post visible, size shots on a pink ruler, wood display stand shots, real selfies of customers and the team wearing the earrings.

**Why:** a consistent look makes a small brand recognizable in a busy feed.

**How to apply:** ad stills and social graphics use these colors and fonts. Keep real product photos as they are; never recolor or redraw an earring. Fonts and logo files go in `ads/assets/` (not committed if licensing is unclear).

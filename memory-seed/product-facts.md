* Hooks and posts: polycarbonate (Wonderlite PC-110). Backs: thermoplastic elastomer and silicone. All latex free.
* Studs come with two pairs of backs, one small and one large.
* Dangles have curved, flexible plastic hooks and no backs. If they catch on a jacket or a little one pulls, they thread out instead of tearing the ear.
* Polycarbonate is "the same material used in safety goggles, motorcycle helmets, and yes, bulletproof glass."
* Assembled by hand in Oklahoma and inspected before shipping.
* Care: do not sleep, shower or swim in them (this also voids the warranty). Not for new piercings. Not for very young children.
* Blanks: clear plastic hooks and posts sold on their own so customers can rehook old earrings.
* Ships same day for orders by 4pm Central, Monday to Friday. Check the shipping policy before quoting delivery times; the FAQ and the policy page give different ranges.

Drafts, research, mockups and plans are free. Anything that leaves this computer or changes something live (a post, an email, an ad, a budget, a price, a product, the theme) needs the owner's yes to that exact item. The request goes in `approvals/`.

**Why:** the owner's name and money are on every one of these, and a mistake in public cannot be unsent.

**How to apply:** prepare the work completely, write the approval file, and stop. Act only after its status is `approved`, then record the approval in the ledger. See the Safety boundaries in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

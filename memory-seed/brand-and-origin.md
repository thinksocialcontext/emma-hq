Earrings by Emma was started by Sarah (Mom) and Emma (then 8 years old) in 2017. Both have ears that react to most metals. Gold worked but was expensive, and Emma lost a gold pair swimming. Sarah remembered wearing plastic post earrings in the 80s without irritation, and the two began making their own. It started as a home school project, sold first from a salon display, then pop ups and fairs, then the website. It is now a full time family business in Oklahoma with a small team of earring makers.

Site footer line: "Family owned, family run, and made with sensitive ears in mind."

**Why:** the origin story is the brand. It is the reason customers trust a small shop over a big jewelry store.

**How to apply:** tell the story in the family's own warm, punny voice. Do not invent new details, quotes or names. Do not name people in photos unless the owner confirms who they are. See [[customers-and-voice]] for tone.

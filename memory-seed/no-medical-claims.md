description: Product claims use only Earrings by Emma's own site wording, with its hedges; never promise a cure, healing or zero reactions.

Say only what the site itself says, in its words. The safest and strongest line is **"No metal touching the ear."** Other approved forms from the site: "100% metal free plastic posts and hooks", "hypoallergenic plastic posts", "It's rare to be allergic to plastic posts because there is no metal to irritate."

Never write: guaranteed no reaction, cures or heals allergies, safe for everyone, doctor recommended, safe for new piercings, or any statistic about allergies.

Keep the site's own hedges whenever a claim needs them: "for the vast majority of people with metal sensitivities"; in very rare cases people react to specific plastics or adhesives; not recommended for new piercings; not for very young children (small parts); posts are a little thicker and can feel snug the first day or two.

The earring faces can be metal or metal look ("Silver", "Gold" variants). The no metal claim is about the post and hook, the part in the ear.

**Why:** health claims on jewelry ads get ads rejected and erode trust with exactly the customers who have been burned before. Meta reviews health language strictly.

**How to apply:** every ad, caption, product description and email passes this check before the critic gate. When unsure, quote the site and link the page. See [[product-facts]].

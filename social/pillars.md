# Content pillars

Five pillars, roughly one post each per week. Each comes from what customers already say.

1. **"I can wear earrings again."** Real reviews (as shown on the site) turned into quote cards, stitched reactions, and "years since I wore earrings" counters.
2. **Behind the scenes.** Gluing posts, bending hooks, folding boxes, filling an order, trade show setup. The family business is the brand.
3. **Plastic post 101.** Why no metal touches the ear, studs versus dangles, two backs included, flexible hooks that thread out, care tips (take them out at night), not for new piercings.
4. **Themes and seasons.** Holidays, back to school, teachers, Classical Conversations cycles, sports seasons, gift guides.
5. **Mom and me.** Parents of kids with sensitive ears, and the moms who then realize their own ears are sensitive too.

Rotate formats: Reels and Shorts first, then carousels, then single images and stories.

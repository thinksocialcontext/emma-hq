---
description: Save a fact or rule the owner just gave as a memory file and index it
argument-hint: "[the fact or rule]"
---

Follow `docs/memory.md`. Input: $ARGUMENTS

1. Search `memory/` for an existing memory on the same subject. Update it rather than adding a duplicate.
2. Otherwise create `memory/<short-kebab-name>.md` with the frontmatter (name, description, type) and the body: the fact or rule, a **Why:** line, a **How to apply:** line, and `[[links]]` to related memories.
3. Add or update its one line in `memory/MEMORY.md`.
4. Never store a password, token, customer's personal details, or anything the owner asked to keep out.
5. Tell the owner in one sentence what you saved.

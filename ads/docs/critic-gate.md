# The critic gate

The owner only ever sees work that has already passed. A different agent (or a fresh session) checks every still against this list and writes `batches/<date>/critic.md` with a pass or fail per ad and the reason.

1. **Right product.** The earring in the photo is the product named in the copy and the link.
2. **Text.** Spelled right, readable at phone size, nothing cut off, no dashes.
3. **Logo.** Correct, not stretched, not recolored.
4. **Claims.** Every claim appears on the site in the same strength. No medical promises. Offers are live today.
5. **Reviews.** Every quote exists on the site, with the name as shown there.
6. **Variety.** No two ads share a layout and background.

Anything that fails is fixed and checked again. Only a clean pass goes into the approval request.
